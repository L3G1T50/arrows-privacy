Privacy Policy

Arrows: Sakura Samurai

Effective date: August 7, 2026

This policy covers Arrows: Sakura Samurai (com.djxl3.arrows), a single-player, offline logic puzzle game published on Google Play. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own — everything below concerns the small amount of data the app's ad and billing SDKs handle on Google's infrastructure.

What the app itself stores

All game state — campaign progress, daily-challenge streak, unlocked skins and bosses, hints balance, and purchase entitlement — is stored only on your device, using Android's DataStore. None of it is transmitted to us or to any third party; we have no server to send it to. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows interstitial ads (between levels) and optional rewarded ads (watched by choice, for an extra hint or life). These are served by Google AdMob, which — depending on your region and consent choice — may collect:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent device identifier)
  • Device and app diagnostic information (device model, OS version, crash/performance data)
  • Approximate, coarse location derived from IP address

This data is used to serve and measure ads and is handled under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Consent (EEA / UK). On first launch, players in the EEA and UK see a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any personalized ad is requested. You can change that choice at any time from the main menu's Privacy options entry.

Purchasing "Remove Ads" stops interstitial ads. Rewarded ads remain available afterward because they're opt-in — you choose to watch one for a hint or an extra life — and this is disclosed at the point of purchase.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google is the merchant of record: we never see or store your payment method, card number, or billing address. The app only receives confirmation that the entitlement was purchased, which it stores on-device as described above. If you reinstall or switch devices, the app can restore this entitlement by asking Google Play what you've already bought.

What we don't do

  • No account creation, no login, no linked identity of any kind.
  • No analytics SDK is included in the app.
  • No data is sold.
  • No data is shared with anyone beyond the AdMob and Play Billing processing described above.

Children's privacy

Arrows: Sakura Samurai is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

This section is written for the general-audience listing. If the Play Console target-audience declaration is later set to include "Under 13," this policy and the app's ad configuration both need to be revisited for Families Policy compliance.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
